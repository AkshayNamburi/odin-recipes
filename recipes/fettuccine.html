Chicken Fettuccine Recipe

Description:

This is a homemade chicken fettuccine recipe that is both easy to cook and delicious. It is a versatile dish that allows you to add your own toppings and seasonings to make it your own. The creamy sauce and tender chicken make for a comforting meal that can be enjoyed any day of the week.

Serve with a side of garlic bread and a salad for a complete meal.

Ingredients (Four servings):

  • 2 tablespoons butter
  • 3 skinless, boneless chicken breast halves (cut into chunks)
  • 1 teaspoon garlic salt
  • 1 1/2 cups heavy cream
  • 1/4 cup grated Parmesan cheese
  • 8 ounces mushrooms, sliced
  • 8 ounces fettuccine pasta
  • 1/8 teaspoon ground black pepper

Steps:

  1. Bring a large pot of lightly salted water to a boil.
  2. Add the 8 ounces of fettuccine for about 8-10 minutes (or until al dente). Drain and set aside.
  3. While the pasta is cooking, heat butter in a large skillet and brown mushrooms and chicken until the chicken is cooked through.
  4. Season with garlic salt and black pepper. Add whipping cream and cook until thickened. Continue to add parmasan cheese at a desired consistency.
  5. Finally, combine the cooked pasta with the sauce and serve.
